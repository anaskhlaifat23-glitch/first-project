      Why applications really get refused

      🏠

      Weak ties to home

      The #1 refusal reason. Officers must believe you'll return: job, property, family and business evidence decide this — most applicants simply don't include enough of it.

      💶

      Wrong funds math

      Every country sets its own funds-per-day rule (Germany ≈ €45, Spain ≈ €108, France up to €120). Showing “a healthy balance” isn't enough — it must clear their number for your trip length.

      🏦

      “Show money” deposits

      A big deposit last week reads as borrowed money. Statements need 3–6 months of natural history — or documented explanations.

      🗺️

      Unclear travel plans

      No reservations, vague itineraries and mismatched dates read as an unclear purpose of travel — an official refusal ground.

      📄

      Missing documents

      Each consulate wants a precise set, in a precise order. One missing paper can end a 46%-risk application.

      🔁

      Unaddressed past refusals

      Consulates share refusal data. A hidden or unexplained previous refusal is close to an automatic no.

      Frequently asked questions

      Is the risk score accurate?

      The score is an estimate built from the EU Commission's published refusal statistics for your nationality (2024–2025) adjusted by the file-strength factors consulates themselves cite: ties, funds, travel history, and clarity of purpose. It cannot predict an individual decision — it shows how your file compares to why applications actually get refused, and what to fix.

      Is it really free?

      Yes — the risk check, red flags and fix-list are free with no signup. We earn a commission if you buy your (mandatory) travel insurance through our partner link, and we sell an optional $9 document pack. Your answers never leave your browser.

      My risk is high — should I still apply?

      Usually not immediately. Most red flags (funds, employment letters, statement history, reservations) take 1–3 months to fix and dramatically change your file. Applying with a weak file costs the full fee and adds a refusal to your record.

      Do you support UK / US / Canada visas?

      Schengen first — it has the clearest published rules. UK and US visitor-visa modules are next on the roadmap.

      Where does my data go?

      Nowhere. The checker runs entirely in your browser; your answers are never uploaded to any server.
