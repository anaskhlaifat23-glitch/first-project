## R3
AI can sound 100% sure and still be wrong 👇

Before anything AI wrote leaves your desk, ask it:
1️⃣ "Which parts of your answer are you least sure about?"
2️⃣ "List your sources for every claim." Then open them yourself.
3️⃣ "Show the calculation step by step for every number."

Fast is good. Checked is better.

## R2
Your AI didn't break. The chat got too long 👇

In very long chats, AI can lose track of early details. Two fixes:

1️⃣ Before a key request:
"Reminder: the goal is [goal], the audience is [who], and [the rule that must not break]."

2️⃣ Start fresh without losing progress:
"Write a handoff summary under 200 words: my goal, decisions made and why, key facts, what is done and what is left."
Then paste it into a new chat.

## R4
Stop asking AI "is my idea good?" It's too polite 👇

Ask this instead:
"Here is my plan: [paste]. Find the three weakest points, explain why each could fail, and what would fix it. Do not praise it."

Praise feels good. Critique saves money.

## R5
Your new tutor is one prompt away 👇

Copy this:
"Teach me [topic] in 5 short steps. After each step, quiz me with one question and wait for my answer. If I get it wrong, explain it differently."

It quizzes you. It waits. It adjusts. Try it with Excel, a new language, anything.

## R1
"Make it better" means nothing to AI 👇

Tell it what better looks like:
1️⃣ "Make it shorter, warmer, and end with one clear question."
2️⃣ "Give me 3 versions: formal, friendly, and blunt."
3️⃣ "Before you answer, ask me 3 questions you need answered first."

Vague in, vague out. Specific in, done.
