Frequently asked questions

Which school of fiqh does the calculator follow?

The shares implemented (spouse, children, parents, grandparents, full and maternal siblings, 'awl, radd, the 'Umariyyah cases) follow the positions agreed upon or held by the majority of Sunni schools. Where schools genuinely differ — such as the grandfather inheriting alongside siblings — the tool flags the difference and recommends consulting a scholar rather than silently picking a side.

Is the generated will legally valid?

The will pack produces a complete, witness-ready document with country-specific signing guidance (e.g. two adult witnesses who are not beneficiaries, in England & Wales and most US states). Validity requirements are jurisdiction-specific — for large or complex estates we recommend having a local lawyer review the signed document.

Why is the spouse's share so small?

This surprises almost everyone — a wife receives 1/8 when there are children, a husband 1/4. This is exactly why running the numbers matters: within your wasiyyah third and lifetime gifts there are fully halal ways to provide more for your spouse, and the will pack explains them.

What about half-siblings, grandchildren, or more distant relatives?

The current version covers the most common heir set. Paternal half-siblings, grandchildren of predeceased sons, and dhawu al-arham cases are on the roadmap — for those estates, consult a scholar today.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. The calculator runs entirely in your browser; nothing you enter is transmitted or stored on any server.
