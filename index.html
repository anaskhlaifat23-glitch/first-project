Frequently asked questions

Is this invoice valid for ZATCA (Saudi Arabia)?

Yes for Phase 1 (Generation): simplified tax invoices include the mandatory QR code encoded in the official TLV/Base64 format with seller name, VAT number, timestamp, total and VAT amount. Phase 2 (Integration with the Fatoora portal) applies to businesses above the announced turnover waves and is on our Business-tier roadmap.

Do I need to create an account?

No. The generator is free and requires no signup. Your details are stored only on your own device so they are pre-filled next time.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. The app runs entirely in your browser — invoice data is never transmitted to any server, which makes it safe for sensitive financial information.

How do I download the PDF?

Click “Download PDF / Print” and choose “Save as PDF” in your browser's print dialog. The output is a clean A4 invoice without the app interface.

Which countries are supported?

Saudi Arabia, UAE, Bahrain, Oman, Qatar, Kuwait, Jordan and Egypt have one-click VAT presets, and you can set any custom rate and currency for other countries.
